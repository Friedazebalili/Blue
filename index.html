What you can hand me.

You don't need to know the technical side. Tell me what's not working, and I'll take it from there.

01

Product pages

Pages that answer questions before customers ask them: photos, ingredients, sizes, reviews and shipping, right where people look. I built these for a group of beauty stores across the UK, Poland, Sweden, New Zealand and Australia.

02

Variants & options

Colour swatches, size pickers and bundles, the parts that get confusing fast when one product comes in twelve versions. I designed these for eight pet stores in Europe, the UK and Oceania.

03

Full store builds

New stores, redesigns, and moves from WooCommerce or Wix, set up so your team can edit everything themselves afterwards.

04

Fixes & speed

Slow pages, broken carts, apps fighting each other, mobile layouts that fall apart. The small stuff that quietly costs you sales.

How it goes.

No long calls or confusing proposals. Just four steps.

Send me your store

A link and a few lines on what you want changed or what's broken.

Get a plan and a price

What I'll do, how long it takes, what it costs. Nothing starts until you say yes.

I work on a copy

Your live store keeps selling while I build. You get a preview link to check along the way.

We go live

We publish together, I show your team how to edit it, and I'm around if anything comes up.
